Hotel Policies

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in from 2:00 PM, check-out by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are available as paid add-ons, subject to availability.

Cancellations

Bookings may be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a one-night charge.

Identification

A valid government-issued ID is required at check-in for every guest listed on the booking.

Pets & Smoking

Pets are welcome in designated rooms only. Smoking is permitted solely in rooms marked smoking-allowed.

Information We Collect

We collect the details you provide when registering, booking, or contacting us — name, contact details, and payment information — solely to manage your stay.

How We Protect It

Passwords are hashed before storage, sessions are secured, and payment data is handled over encrypted connections. We do not sell guest information to third parties.

Your Choices

You may update or request deletion of your profile information at any time from your dashboard, or by contacting our support team.

Bookings

A booking is confirmed once payment is received or a valid payment method is held. Rates are shown per night and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

Liability

Siddartha Palace is not liable for loss of personal property except where required by law. Guests are responsible for any damage caused to hotel property.

Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms from time to time; the version in effect at the time of your booking applies to that stay.
