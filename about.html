About Siddartha Palace

Our Story

Ninety years of quiet hospitality

Siddartha Palace began as the royal residence in 1932. Over the decades, its courtyards and halls were opened to travellers, restored with authentic care rather than replaced, so that the palace's character remains intact.

Today it operates as a full-service heritage hotel — 92 rooms and suites across four floors, two restaurants, an Ayurvedic wellness spa, and event halls — while keeping the same royal warmth the family once gave their own guests.
