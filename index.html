The Palace

Where heritage architecture meets modern rest

Once the residence of the Siddartha royal family, the palace has been restored stone by stone into a hotel that honours its past. Hand-painted ceilings, jharokha balconies, and courtyard fountains sit alongside quiet, contemporary interiors designed for a genuine night's rest.

Every stay is looked after by a dedicated concierge team, from the moment you book online to the moment you check out.
